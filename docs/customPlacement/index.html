Promotion Widget Not Showing in the Cart Drawer

Custom Placement allows you to display the widget at a specific location on your website.

Info

If your promotion widget is not appearing inside your cart drawer, your store is most likely using a custom Shopify theme.

Many custom themes use a unique cart drawer implementation, so our app cannot automatically determine the best location to display the widget. This can be resolved in just a few minutes.

Solution: Enable Custom Placement

After enabling Custom Placement, you'll be able to place the promotion widget exactly where you want it within your theme.

Need Help? We'll Do It for You

Free Setup Service

If you're not comfortable editing your theme, our team is happy to set everything up for you—free of charge.

Simply grant us Shopify Collaborator Access, and we'll:

  • Find the correct cart drawer file in your theme.
  • Insert the required widget snippet.
  • Verify that the promotion widget displays correctly.
  • Ensure everything works as expected before we're done.

Most installations are completed within a few minutes.

Why This Is Required

Note

Every Shopify theme is built differently. While Yaki automatically integrates with most popular themes, some custom or heavily modified themes require a one-time manual placement of the widget.

Once configured, the widget will continue working normally, and no further setup is required.
